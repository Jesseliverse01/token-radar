Live-token protocols are the comparables, not the radar's subjects. Their median mcap / TVL, category by category, is what prices the pre-token board.

How the radar prices a protocol

implied value = TVL × category multiple × growth × maturity × breadth

The multiple is the median market-cap / TVL of the live-token protocols in the same category (at least three of them; otherwise the global median across all live protocols). Growth is the 7-day TVL change, clamped to −50%…+100% and weighted 0.35. Maturity runs 0.75 for a new protocol to 1.00 at three years. Breadth adds 2% per extra chain, up to four. Nothing here is a name list: change the snapshot's figures and the ranking changes with them.

What counts as pre-token

A protocol with no token at all — no market cap, no CoinGecko id, no symbol — that is not a centralised exchange, not a wrapped, staked or bridged product of a token that already trades, and not a versioned copy of another protocol. That is why WBTC, Binance staked ETH and USDT0 are not on this board, even though their TVL is enormous: each is a product of a token that already trades.

What the farm plan is

Seven concrete steps per protocol: what to do, what it costs, how long it takes. Capital scales with the protocol's size, gas follows the chain it lives on, and the hold length follows how old it is. A farm plan earns a position; it does not promise a token.
